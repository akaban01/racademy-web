General expectations

  • All clothing must be modest, neat, and appropriately fitted.
  • Uniform items should be free of graphics or decorative designs unless specifically approved.
  • Students who are out of uniform may be subject to school dress code procedures.
Questions about a specific item? Sizing, color matches, or replacements between school years - please reach out to the office at contact@racademy.org or 512-252-2277.
