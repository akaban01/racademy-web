Program pillars

Preschool at Renaissance Academy is built around three core goals: helping children feel secure, grow in confidence, and prepare for future academic success with joy.

Faith & character

  • Morning greetings and Islamic manners
  • Simple Qur’an and dua exposure
  • Kindness, patience, and responsibility

Early academics

  • Language and vocabulary growth
  • Pre-writing, counting, and problem-solving
  • Music, stories, and conversation-rich learning

Social & motor growth

  • Friendship building and classroom routines
  • Fine and gross motor activities
  • Outdoor movement and healthy habits

Preschool schedule at a glance

Children benefit from a predictable rhythm that balances learning, movement, rest, and connection with caring adults.

Time Focus
Morning Arrival, greetings, circle time, and a gentle transition into play-based learning.
Midday Small group lessons, language development, music, snack, and guided activity blocks.
Afternoon Outdoor exploration, rest/quiet time, and social learning through centers and stories.
Dismissal Reflection, calm transitions, and end-of-day communication with families.
Step 1 Greeting the day

Children settle in with routines, songs, and a calm start.

Step 2 Learning blocks

Teachers guide purposeful play, books, and early academic practice.

Step 3 Movement & snack

Outdoor time, healthy habits, and social interaction support growth.

Step 4 Closing circle

Families receive a warm wrap-up and a clear sense of the day’s progress.
