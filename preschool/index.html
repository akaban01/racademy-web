Preschool · Faith · Play · Early Learning

Where young learners begin with joy, routine, and purpose.

Our preschool program creates a warm, engaging first step for children as they learn to greet the day, build friendships, and explore the foundations of Islam, language, and early academics.

What families can expect

  • Gentle, age-appropriate learning in a caring classroom setting
  • Daily Islamic values, Qur’an exposure, and Arabic rhythm
  • Hands-on play, movement, stories, and circle time
  • Strong communication with teachers and family support

At a glance

A preschool experience designed for early growth.

The program combines academic readiness with emotional security, helping children take first steps in a structured and loving environment.

Faith-centered
Daily habits, manners, and Islamic values woven into classroom life.
Play-based
Learning through centers, stories, movement, and hands-on exploration.
Warm routines
Predictable rhythms help children feel safe and confident.
Strong support
Teachers partner closely with families to support each child’s growth.

Program pillars

Preschool at Renaissance Academy is built around three core goals: helping children feel secure, grow in confidence, and prepare for future academic success with joy.

Faith & character

  • Morning greetings and Islamic manners
  • Simple Qur’an and dua exposure
  • Kindness, patience, and responsibility

Early academics

  • Language and vocabulary growth
  • Pre-writing, counting, and problem-solving
  • Music, stories, and conversation-rich learning

Social & motor growth

  • Friendship building and classroom routines
  • Fine and gross motor activities
  • Outdoor movement and healthy habits

Preschool schedule at a glance

Children benefit from a predictable rhythm that balances learning, movement, rest, and connection with caring adults.

Time Focus
Morning Arrival, greetings, circle time, and a gentle transition into play-based learning.
Midday Small group lessons, language development, music, snack, and guided activity blocks.
Afternoon Outdoor exploration, rest/quiet time, and social learning through centers and stories.
Dismissal Reflection, calm transitions, and end-of-day communication with families.
Step 1 Greeting the day

Children settle in with routines, songs, and a calm start.

Step 2 Learning blocks

Teachers guide purposeful play, books, and early academic practice.

Step 3 Movement & snack

Outdoor time, healthy habits, and social interaction support growth.

Step 4 Closing circle

Families receive a warm wrap-up and a clear sense of the day’s progress.

Arrival, dismissal, and Monday early pick-up

The daily rhythm is the same for every preschool classroom, with one change families should plan around: every Monday is an early pick-up day for preschool and the big school, all year long.

When What happens
7:40–8:00 AM Arrival every day. Please tell the teacher about changes at home, a difficult night, or an early pick-up.
2:30–4:00 PM Mondays only — early pick-up window for preschool and the big school, throughout the school year.
3:25–3:50 PM Regular dismissal Tuesday through Friday. Teachers read stories until the last child is picked up.
3:50–4:00 PM Late arrivals at pick-up. At 4:00 PM afterschool children move to the PK4 room and classroom teachers go home.
From Aug 17 The afterschool program begins and runs until 5:00 PM — including Mondays.

The first two weeks for new children

Preschoolers are as young as two, and for many this is the first time away from home. New children ease in over two weeks with short days that grow a little longer each day. Please plan the first two weeks so you can follow this schedule — it makes the transition far smoother. Returning children follow the regular day from August 10.
